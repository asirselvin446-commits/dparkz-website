# Brag Plan: D'PARKZ

## What is this app?
D'PARKZ is a cinematic single-page restaurant website for an Indo-Chinese, tandoor and biryani restaurant in Thakkamalpuram, Tamil Nadu — featuring a scroll-driven 240-frame walk-in animation, horizontal dish gallery with 3D tilt cards, parallax photo gallery, and an immersive atmosphere section, all in one HTML file.

## The angle
The website *is* the walk-in. You scroll, you walk through the garden entrance, past the flower ceiling, to the table. The brag video recreates that journey in 20 seconds: the warm dark palette, the serif typography, the food photography, and the tagline that anchors everything — "Good food. Good company. Good memories." This isn't a tech demo. It's a restaurant that made its website feel like arriving.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dark screen. The D'PARKZ wordmark fades in — Playfair Display, warm paper color on night (#1B120B). Beneath it, the label "Restaurant · Thakkamalpuram" appears. The warmth of the palette is immediate and sets the mood before a single dish is shown.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The walk-in moment:** A warm-toned frame from the walk-in sequence (the dining room with flower ceiling and gold lamps), revealed with a slow scale and grain overlay — capturing the site's signature scroll experience as a single cinematic beat.
- **The dishes:** Dynamite Chicken and Mutton Biryani photos slide in as polaroid-style print cards (referencing the site's `.print` gallery cards), each with a serif caption. The food photography speaks for itself.
- **The atmosphere line:** "Where flavour meets ambience." — full-screen serif italic, over a softened room photo, echoing the site's atmosphere band section.

## Outro / punchline
The 4.6 rating appears large in Playfair Display with "from 5,078 reviews" beneath it — then crossfades to the final tagline: "Good food. Good company. Good memories." with the D'PARKZ wordmark. Hold. Confidence through stillness.

## User flow worth showing
None — landing-page-only static restaurant site. The strongest material is the visual identity, the food photography, and the atmosphere, not an app flow.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: warm premium restaurant film — candlelight confidence
- Interpretation: Fewer scenes, longer holds. The product is beautiful and doesn't need to rush. Restraint in motion, warmth in palette. Every transition is a slow crossfade. The serif typography does the heavy lifting.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #1B120B (night)
- Accent: #B96B3E (orange), #2F6F6B (teal)
- Text: #F3E5C7 (paper-lt)
- Secondary surface: #E8D5B0 (paper)
- Display font: Playfair Display (serif, 700)
- Body font: Manrope (sans, 400-800)
- Strongest visual element: The walk-in scroll animation, the dark/warm palette, the food photography, the grain texture overlay, the polaroid-style gallery prints

## Share copy (draft)
D'PARKZ, Thakkamalpuram. Indo-Chinese starters, tandoor, biryani, and a ceiling of flowers. Rated 4.6 from 5,078 guests. Good food. Good company. Good memories.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm cinematic bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (steady and clean, ~110 BPM)
- Music treatment: fade in from 0s at volume 0.32; let it breathe under the visuals; gentle fade-out starting at ~18s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset; tempo ~110 BPM. Strong cues at 8.74s and 13.11s for dish reveals; strong cue at 17.47s for the rating reveal. Use sparingly — this is a polished video.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS/bass to make the background warmth and grain breathe gently. No waveform/equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse / polished restraint; 2-3 quiet cues maximum
- Audio-coupled moments: dish card arrivals (soft wood or drop sound), rating reveal (gentle bell), final tagline (let the music carry it alone)
- Restraint rule: no aggressive impacts, no stacking, no more than 3 SFX total. Let silence and the music bed do the emotional work.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Arrival — 4s
Dark background (#1B120B) with a subtle grain overlay. The "D'PARKZ" wordmark fades in large — Playfair Display, 700, #F3E5C7. Below it, "Restaurant · Thakkamalpuram" appears as a small uppercase label in Manrope. Slow, confident, warm.
Sequential/interaction: none — single fade-in of wordmark, then label
Audio intent: warmth, anticipation — the music bed starts here
Audio-coupled idea: none — let the music intro carry
Music: warm fade-in, building gently
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Room — 4s
A full-bleed photo of the D'PARKZ dining room (photo-room.webp) with a radial vignette, slow parallax scale (1.02→1.0). Overlay text: "Where flavour meets ambience." in Playfair Display italic, centered, #F3E5C7. The grain texture continues.
Sequential/interaction: none — photo with text overlay
Audio intent: immersion, presence — music continues, building
Audio-coupled idea: none — visual moment
Music: steady bed, slightly more present
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The Plates — 5s
Two dish photos (Dynamite Chicken and Mutton Biryani) enter as polaroid-style cards on the dark background — each with a cream (#E8D5B0) border, slight rotation, and a serif caption beneath. They arrive one after the other with a gentle stagger.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two dish cards arrive one by one, ~1.5s apart
Audio intent: each card arrival is a small moment — warm, tactile
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop or wood knock sound on each card landing
Music: beat-aligned card arrivals if possible (beats near 8.74s and 9.83s)
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The Rating — 3.5s
Large "4.6" in Playfair Display, amber/orange (#B96B3E), with "from 5,078 reviews" in Manrope beneath it, centered on the dark background. Five star icons appear. Clean, confident.
Sequential/interaction: none — stat appears as a block
Audio intent: quiet achievement — one gentle bell to mark the reveal
Audio-coupled idea: soft bell on the rating number appearing
Music: steady presence
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Good Memories — 3.5s
The tagline in three lines, centered, Playfair Display:
"Good food."
"Good company."
"Good memories."
Each line appears gently. Below, the D'PARKZ wordmark in teal (#2F6F6B). Hold. Breathe.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three lines arrive one by one, then the wordmark
Audio intent: resolution, warmth — the final emotional beat
Audio-coupled idea: none — let the music fade gently carry the ending
Music: gentle fade-out begins
Transition mood: end
